## Template library

Most of the ~220 templates bundled under `templates/` (background images
and layout data) were imported from
[jacebrowning/memegen](https://github.com/jacebrowning/memegen), an MIT
licensed open-source project. As with upstream memegen, each imported
template's `source` field is a provenance pointer to the meme's origin
(e.g. a Know Your Meme page), not a copyright clearance — memegen itself
doesn't claim per-image rights either, and operates on the same widely
accepted parody/commentary norms this project follows. See
[`TEMPLATES.md`](TEMPLATES.md) for the full breakdown, including the
smaller set of original/public-domain/CC-licensed templates that carry
their own specific attribution.

A handful of templates (`prison-mike`, `kevins-chili`, `creed`) are TV
screencaps from *The Office* (NBC, 2005-2013), sourced directly rather than
via memegen since memegen's own catalog only carries three Office templates.
These sit on the same legal footing as the memegen imports above: fair-use
parody screencaps, not a rights clearance.
